## Description (100–1000) — 780

```
You wake up in an old bunker on a wild voxel planet. You have nothing, and around you are forests, mountains, caves and ore deposits that never run out.

At first you do everything by hand: chop trees, craft a pickaxe at the workbench, forge your first parts in mini-games. Every process you do by hand unlocks its automation: steam machines, drills on deposits, inserters and conveyors, electricity and a storage network with autocrafting.

• A huge world: 15 biomes, rivers, mountains, floating islands, ruins and caves.
• Knowledge tree and eras: wood, stone, iron, steam, electricity.
• Machines with configurable sides, pipes, cables and belts.
• Animals for food and farming, night predators with special attacks.
• Map with waypoints, recipe book and production statistics.
```

## How to play (100–1000) — 495

```
Mouse — look around, W A S D — move, Space — jump, Shift — sprint.
Hold LMB — break a block or hit a creature, RMB — place a block, open a machine, put an item on a belt, eat.
1–9 and mouse wheel — select item, Q — drop, Shift+Q — drop the stack.
E — inventory with a 2×2 crafting grid; the workbench has a 3×3 grid. R — rotate a machine or belt.
K — knowledge tree: it shows what to do next. J — recipe book, M — map, P — statistics.
Esc — pause (frees the mouse). The game saves automatically.
```
