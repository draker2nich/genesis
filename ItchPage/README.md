## Description

```
Wake up in an old bunker on a wild voxel planet and turn it into a working factory.

Start with your bare hands: chop trees, craft a pickaxe at the workbench and forge your first parts in quick mini-games. Every process you do by hand unlocks its automation — steam drills on endless ore deposits, conveyor belts, robotic arms, electricity and a storage network that crafts for you.

FEATURES
• A huge world with 15 biomes, rivers, mountains, floating islands, caves and ruins
• Eras from wood and stone to steam and electricity, guided by a knowledge tree
• Machines, belts, pipes and cables you connect yourself
• Animals to farm and night creatures that keep you on your toes
• Map with waypoints, recipe book and production stats
• Your world is saved automatically in the browser
• English and Russian

CONTROLS
Mouse – look · WASD – move · Space – jump · Shift – sprint
Hold left click – mine / hit · Right click – place, open machine, eat
1–9 / wheel – choose item · E – inventory · J – recipes · K – what to do next
M – map · R – rotate machine · Q – drop · Esc – pause
On phones and tablets: on-screen joystick and buttons (landscape).

The game is in active development — feedback in the comments is very welcome!
Works best in Chrome, Firefox or Edge on a desktop.
```
