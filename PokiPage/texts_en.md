
## Description

```
Wake up in an old bunker on a wild voxel planet and turn it into a working factory.

Start with your bare hands: chop trees, craft a pickaxe at the workbench and forge your first parts in quick mini-games. Every process you do by hand unlocks its automation — steam drills on endless ore deposits, conveyor belts, robotic arms, electricity and a storage network that crafts for you.

• A huge world with 15 biomes, rivers, mountains, floating islands, caves and ruins
• Eras from wood and stone to steam and electricity, guided by a knowledge tree
• Machines, belts, pipes and cables you connect yourself
• Animals to farm and night creatures that keep you on your toes
• Map with waypoints, recipe book and production stats
• Your world is saved automatically in the browser
```

## Controls — desktop

```
Mouse – look around · WASD – move · Space – jump · Shift – sprint
Hold left click – mine / hit · Right click – place block, open machine, eat
1–9 or mouse wheel – choose item · E – inventory and crafting · J – recipes
K – what to do next · M – map · R – rotate machine · Q – drop item
Esc – pause
```

## Controls — mobile and tablet

```
Left side of the screen – joystick to walk (push to the edge to run)
Right side – drag to look around · Tap – place block / open machine / eat
Touch and hold – mine or hit · Buttons – jump, down, bag, recipes, map, pause
Tap an item in the hotbar to choose it
```

## Notes for the review form

- Platforms: desktop, mobile, tablet (touch controls are built in).
- Orientation on mobile: landscape.
- Ads: commercialBreak() before entering a world and when resuming from the pause menu; no rewarded ads.
- Saves: automatic, browser storage (IndexedDB); progress survives page reloads.
- No external requests, links, in-game purchases or other ad networks.
- Languages: English and Russian (picked from the browser language, switchable in Settings).
